Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 7-day free trial work?

When you create an OXYCORP account, you automatically get 7 days of full access to all features. No payment required upfront. After 7 days, choose a plan to continue.

How do I pay with M-Pesa?

Enter your Safaricom phone number (07XXXXXXXX format) and click "Pay with M-Pesa". You'll receive an STK push prompt on your phone. Enter your M-Pesa PIN to confirm payment.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When upgrading, you'll be charged the difference. When downgrading, the change takes effect at your next billing cycle.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep access until the end of your current billing period. After that, your account reverts to the free tier. Your data is always preserved.

Is my payment information secure?

Absolutely. All payments are processed through Safaricom's official M-Pesa API. OXYCORP never stores your M-Pesa PIN or financial details.
